# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Critique the code, not the person who wrote it.

## What that means

Expected of everyone taking part — issues, pull requests, discussions:

- Be welcoming to people at any level of experience with 3D, graphics or photography.
- Give feedback on the work, with a reason. "This adds a dependency we don't need, and
  `Array.prototype.at` does the same thing" is useful. "This is garbage" is not.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a contribution. This project has deliberate
  constraints, listed in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Respect that the photographs here are someone's copyrighted work.

Not acceptable, anywhere in this project:

- Harassment, insults, or personal, political or identity-based attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Redistributing the photographs in this repository in violation of
  [their licence](photos/LICENSE).

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, reviews — and
anywhere someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by messaging [@ni_sh_a.char on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/ni_sh_a.char/),
or by opening an issue if it can be discussed publicly. Reports are handled privately, and
whoever reports something in good faith will not be penalised for it.

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions that break these rules, and may block
accounts for repeated or severe breaches. Responses are proportionate: usually a warning
first, a block for anything sustained or serious.
