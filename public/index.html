About

A galiyaara is a corridor —
somewhere you pass through
and it passes through you.

Shauk-ai-Deedar hai toh Nazar paida keejiye,
Mayine toh kayi hain Janab sabar paida keejiye.

Every photograph here was made on the move: ghats before sunrise, alleys after the shutters come down, faces that held still for exactly one frame. This gallery is the corridor they all hang in.
